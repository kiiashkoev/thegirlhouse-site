Frequently asked questions

How do I book?

The easiest way is Booksy: you see every stylist's free times and book straight away. You can also call us: 730 853 817.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

In the Booksy app, under your bookings. Please make changes at least 24 hours in advance so another client can take the time.

How long does a gel polish manicure take?

90 to 105 minutes, depending on the service. The exact time of each service is in the price list.

How can I pay?

By card, cash, BLIK or Booksy Pay.

Which languages do you speak?

Polish, English and Ukrainian.

Where can I park?

There is parking in front of the salon.
